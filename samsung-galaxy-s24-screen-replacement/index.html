Galaxy S24 · Screens

Galaxy S24 screen,
back to brilliant.

S24, S24+, S24 Ultra or S24 FE — one of the brightest displays Samsung has ever made shouldn't stay cracked. Most are done the same day.

The S24 generation is when Samsung went all-in on flat displays — even the Ultra lost its curved edges — and packed in one of the brightest screens on any phone. Bright doesn't mean unbreakable, though, and a drop onto concrete doesn't care what year your phone came out. The upside: flat glass makes for a particularly clean, precise screen replacement.

Signs your Galaxy S24 screen needs attention

  • Cracked or spider-webbed glass, from a corner chip to the full web
  • Patches of the screen that ignore your touch
  • Green or pink lines, flickering, or washed-out colours after a drop
  • A display that stays black even though the phone still rings and buzzes

How we put it right

Every job starts with a free diagnosis — we confirm whether it's the display itself or something smaller, and give you a clear, honest quote for your exact model before any work begins. If it needs replacing, we fit a quality AMOLED display, keep your fingerprint reader working as it should, and test touch, colour and brightness before handover. On the S24 Ultra we also check the S Pen behaves exactly as before.

Galaxy AI, your photos, your setup — none of it is touched by a screen repair. The phone that comes back to you is your phone, just without the cracks.

Why fix rather than upgrade

An S24 has years of updates ahead of it — Samsung's longest software promise yet. A fresh screen costs a fraction of a new phone, keeps everything exactly as you like it, and leaves the bench with our standard repair warranty on the work.
